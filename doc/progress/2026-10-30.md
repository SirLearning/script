## 2026-10-30 — lowcov_vs_homsnp on `test_common_only` (WAP + Watkins)

- Replotted the depth/missingness-vs-hom-SNP scatter for both combine cohorts on `test_common_only`, using the pair tables from 69/73 and the same parameters as the `test_common_inter` 52/53 runs: `76run_combine_ibs_wap_vmap3_lowcov_test_common_only` (`pair_type=all`, cutoff 0.995, y 0.96–1.0) and `77run_combine_ibs_watkins_lowcov_test_common_only` (`pair_type=within_watkins`, cutoff 0.99, 48-member groups highlighted).
- Six figures per cohort land in `stats/test_common_only/plots/`: base (log depth), `.linear`, `.high_missing_rate{,.linear}`, `.missing_sum{,.linear}`. No pairs were dropped for missing coverage; all 882 / 4,772 pair samples had a missing rate.
- Spearman correlations are essentially unchanged from `test_common_inter`, so the depth/missingness mechanism is the same:

  | cohort | depth ρ (only / inter) | high-missing ρ | missing-sum ρ | median hom (only / inter) | pass cutoff (only / inter) |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | WAP_PQ/VMap3 | 0.179 / 0.216 | −0.152 / −0.136 | −0.269 / −0.294 | 0.9946 / 0.9986 | 429 / 553 (0.995) |
  | Watkins | 0.787 / 0.792 | −0.929 / −0.926 | −0.977 / −0.977 | 0.9820 / 0.9959 | 2158 / 4769 (0.99) |

- What changes is the **absolute level**, not the slope: on `test_common_only` the whole Watkins cloud sits ~0.014 lower, so at the fixed 0.99 cutoff only 45% pass instead of 99.9%. The per-depth-bin medians show the same shift (0.30–0.50×: 0.9739 vs 0.9940; >3×: 0.9979 vs 0.9995).
- WAP is weakly depth-dependent in both sets (ρ 0.18–0.22) because that cohort mixes true duplicates with non-duplicates; Watkins is dominated by missingness (ρ = −0.98 on the missing-rate sum), and the 48-member groups sit in the high-missingness tail in both sets.
- Consequence for the merge rule: the low-coverage correction learned on `test_common_inter` cannot be applied as an absolute IBS offset on `test_common_only`; the cutoff must move with the test set (or be expressed as a percentile of the random reference), while the missing-sum axis is transferable.

## 2026-10-30 — Combine-pair hom-SNP IBS ranking: test_common_inter vs test_common_only

- Question: does the ordering of combine pairs by hom-SNP IBS change between the two test sets? Joined each cohort's pair tables 1:1 (882 WAP pairs, 4,783 IPK/HZAU/Watkins), ranked by `hom_ibs` descending, and compared ranks, top-k sets, cutoff sides and tiers. Run-local analysis in `16stats.genome/78run_combine_ibs_rank_compare/` (no Nextflow).
- Global ordering is preserved. WAP: Spearman 0.988 all pairs, **0.995 for AABBDD only** (n=835), Kendall τ 0.930. Watkins: Spearman **0.992**, τ 0.930.
- Local reshuffling is modest once ties are excluded: WAP median |rank shift| 12 (1.4% of the list), p90 43; untied subset median 10, p90 32. Watkins median 72 (1.5%), p90 285; untied subset median 23, p90 81. The raw Watkins number is inflated by a large tie mass — 68% of pairs share an IBS value with another pair in at least one set (13% of values are non-unique in `test_common_only`), so average ranks move many tied pairs at once.
- Top of the list is stable but not identical: top-10 overlap 8/10 in both cohorts; top-50 0.80–0.94; top-100 0.89–0.93.
- The one structural change is **ploidy composition in WAP**. Ranking mixes ploidy groups whose `hom_ibs` is averaged over different subgenomes, and `test_common_only` has 2.5× more D variants (590,944 vs 239,339) but 0.8× the B variants (1,153,004 vs 1,454,150). Consequently the 5 DD pairs rise a median 275 ranks, the 40 AABB pairs rise 34, AABBDD pairs are unchanged (+1), and the 2 AA pairs fall 86. Example: `D_0040–D_0253` goes 0.9908 → 0.9990 (rank 596 → 45).
- Reshuffling is **not** a low-coverage artifact: ρ(rank shift, missing-rate sum) = −0.013 for Watkins and −0.23 for WAP; ρ with low coverage 0.07 / 0.38. Per-subgenome deltas explain little either (|ρ| ≤ 0.11), because the whole distribution shifts down together.
- Individual extremes: `CRR877638–CRR877639` falls 1,780 ranks (0.9947 → 0.9625), `CRR878281–CRR878282` rises 1,605 (0.9937 → 0.9787). WAP's largest drops are AABBDD pairs sitting at 0.996–0.999 on inter and 0.992–0.998 on only.
- Practical consequence: ordering is stable, absolute thresholds are not. Tier labels change for 30% of WAP pairs and **73%** of Watkins pairs, and the movement is one-directional — nothing moves up except 2 WAP mid→high, while all 2,114 inter-mid Watkins pairs become low, and 1,098 of 2,668 inter-high pairs become mid. Cutoff-side flips follow the same pattern: 0 only-pass/inter-fail in both cohorts.
