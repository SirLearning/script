## 2026-10-26 — SUPP — IPK/HZAU/Watkins hom-SNP bimodality diagnosed

- The two hom-SNP peaks are the two 48-member Watkins groups, not a biological two-class split. `WATDE0278` (1128 pairs, hom median 0.9942) and `WATDE0305` (1128 pairs, 0.9943) account for 2242/2391 pairs in the 0.990-0.996 peak. The 0.996-1.0 peak is the smaller Watkins groups (median 0.9988) plus the 11 IPK pairs.
- Coverage drives it: the 48-member groups have dedup coverage median 0.48x and 0.58x, while the 2-6 member groups are at 2.12x. Hom-SNP IBS correlates with min coverage at Spearman 0.79; median hom IBS by coverage bin is 0.9944 (<0.75x), 0.9983 (0.75-1.5x), 0.9989 (1.5-3x), 0.9995 (>3x). 2384/2387 pairs in the low peak have coverage <1x.
- Hom-SNP amplifies the effect because it uses only sites where both samples are homozygous; low coverage leaves fewer, noisier hom sites. PLINK IBS separates the same groups by only 0.9915/0.9908 vs 0.9929, while hom-SNP separates them by 0.9942/0.9943 vs 0.9988.
- Excluding the two 48-member groups leaves a tight distribution at 0.998-0.9995; restricting to coverage >=1x gives a main peak at 0.9996. The 48-member groups are still the same dedup group; their lower hom-SNP values are a coverage bias, not evidence against duplicate status.
- Per-subgenome hom medians in the low peak: A 0.9955, B 0.9968, D 0.9905; the high peak is ~0.999 in all three. The D subgenome is the most affected in the low-coverage groups.
